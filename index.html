The area is calculated by rectifying the marked floor plane to the known width and length, then measuring the traced polygon in that plane. It is an estimate; blocked edges, uneven floors, lens distortion, inaccurate corner placement, or non-rectangular calibration can affect it.

How to measure

The more accurate your reference measurements and corner marks, the better the estimate.

  1. Take a wide photo from a doorway or elevated spot. Include the visible floor corners and avoid strong wide-angle distortion.
  2. Choose Mark floor corners, then tap the four corners of the floor area clockwise, starting near-left.
  3. Measure two adjacent floor edges with a tape measure. Enter width and length in the selected unit.
  4. Choose Trace floor boundary and tap around the room’s visible floor perimeter. Close the shape to see the estimate.
Important: a single photo cannot reliably measure a room by itself. This tool needs real measurements to calibrate perspective; if you cannot see a floor edge, ordinary side-on photos may give a poor estimate.
